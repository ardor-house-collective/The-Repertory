New Brand

Structural Profile controls how this brand’s products are organized within The Repertory. It determines whether the Family grouping layer is required, whether the Style layer is available, and how GNS records are assembled when a contributor selects this brand. Choose the option that best describes how the brand structures its own product line internally — this is about product organization, not corporate ownership.

Standalone: one core product or tightly focused line with no sub-groupings.
Aesthetic Family: products grouped by design language or named lines.
Model Iteration: line built around one or a few core silhouettes in many variants.
Mixed: more than one structure across different parts of the line.
Unknown: select if unsure — a curator will assign the correct profile on review.

Getting this right matters — it affects how contributor forms behave for this brand. If you’re not sure, select Unknown.
