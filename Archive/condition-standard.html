Purpose

The Repertory Condition Standard is a canonical grading framework for luxury goods in the secondary market. It is designed to be precise enough for authentication and valuation use, consistent enough to serve as an industry reference, and open enough to be adopted across resale platforms, dealers, and contributors regardless of affiliation.

6 condition tiers
From Pristine to Fair — plus Damaged and Decommissioned designations
4 categories
Handbags · SLGs · Accessories · Jewelry & Watches (frameworks)

Architecture
Condition Grade
TRP-1 through TRP-6 plus Damaged and Decommissioned. Assigned to every object record. Reflects current physical state.
R/A Layer
Repaired/Altered log. Runs alongside any condition grade. Each intervention is a discrete entry with type, performer, date, and documentation status.
Object Status
Active, Damaged, or Decommissioned. Applies to the object, not the listing. Permanent once Decommissioned.

Condition reflects what the object is now. The R/A layer explains how it got there. Object status describes its market viability. These are independent fields — a bag can be TRP-4 Very Good with an R/A flag and Active status simultaneously.

“TRP-1 and TRP-2 are unworn grades. Everything from TRP-3 downward reflects degrees of actual human use.”

In the luxury secondary market, the difference between unworn and lightly used is detectable. Corner break-in, handle oils, interior transfer, and closure wear patterns are introduced by use — not storage. This distinction is the foundation of the Repertory grading system.

Condition Scale — Handbags

All tiers defined for handbags. See category extensions below for SLGs, accessories, jewelry, and watches.

TRP-1 Pristine
Unworn

The bag is new. Never worn, never carried, never used. Presents exactly as it would from retail. Hardware retains full factory finish, leather retains factory luster or coating, protective films or interior tissue may still be intact. Inclusions typically present but their absence alone does not disqualify this grade if all physical criteria are met.

  • Zero corner wear, softening, or lifting of any kind
  • Zero scratches, scuffs, or contact marks on hardware
  • Zero interior marks, ink transfer, odor, or staining
  • Zero handle darkening, glazing cracks, or strap creasing from handling
  • Lining crisp, undisturbed, free of compression marks
  • All original closures function with factory feel
Age prompt triggers at 2 years — “This item is 2+ years old. Please confirm no storage wear is present before selecting Pristine.” Verification prompt only, not a disqualifier.
TRP-2 Exceptional
Unworn

Never worn or carried. Has not been used. However, the passage of time, conditions of storage, or characteristics of its own construction have introduced minor passive changes. The human hand has not worn this bag — but time, environment, or design have left their mark. Accepts three categories of passive wear, none use-introduced: storage wear, handling wear from transfer, and wear inherent to design (WID).

  • Storage wear: press marks from tissue or box, light dust, vachetta tone shift from ambient light, minor humidity effect, compression from flat storage
  • Handling wear from transfer: minimal contact marks from movement of free-hanging metal parts — mechanically unavoidable, not use wear
  • WID marks: structural consequence of construction, not use — see WID definition
  • Corners: fully intact, zero break-in, zero color loss
  • Hardware: hairline micro-scratches from metal-on-metal contact only
  • Handles/straps: full shape retained, zero oil transfer or use patina
  • Interior: clean, lining smooth, no human-introduced marks
WID marks do not lower grade. Never worn · Never carried.
TRP-3 Excellent
Used

Has been carried. Shows light but visible evidence of actual use. Wear is minor and consistent — nothing distracting when worn. All structure, hardware function, and closure integrity fully intact. Highest grade for a bag that has been actively carried.

  • Corners: trace wear only — no bare leather, no color loss beyond surface sheen shift
  • Hardware: light surface scratches from contact; no pitting, no significant oxidation
  • Handles/straps: light surface marks; no oil darkening; shape intact
  • Interior: minimal marking, no odor, no staining; light dust acceptable
  • Glazing/edge paint: intact with minor surface crazing at most
  • No torn lining, broken closures, missing hardware, or structural compromise
TRP-4 Very Good
Used

Moderate use visible on inspection. Bag is undeniably well-maintained — wear is honest and consistent with regular careful carry, not neglect. All hardware and closures function. Nothing requires repair to use or present. Most common grade for actively used bags from careful owners.

  • Corners: noticeable wear; some lightening or bare edge leather acceptable; no fraying
  • Hardware: moderate scratching; early oxidation or tarnish on non-palladium or gold hardware acceptable
  • Handles/straps: moderate use marks; minimal oil darkening; shape retained
  • Interior: light marks and minor staining acceptable; no heavy ink transfer; no strong persistent odor
  • Glazing/edge paint: some chipping or cracking; not actively peeling in large areas
  • No torn lining, broken closures, or missing hardware
R/A ceiling — TRP-4 maximum if manufacturer service only.
TRP-5 Good
Used

Clearly and honestly used. Wear is obvious on visual inspection and distributed across multiple areas. Fully functional and wearable but would not be confused with a well-maintained piece. All flaws must be disclosed and documented.

  • Corners: visible wear with color loss; bare leather; minor fraying acceptable
  • Hardware: heavy scratching; visible oxidation or verdigris; tarnish that cannot be buffed
  • Handles/straps: significant oil darkening; patina buildup; light cracking to glazed leather acceptable
  • Interior: visible staining, ink marks, or transfer; moderate odor if disclosed; lining intact
  • Glazing/edge paint: active peeling or flaking in localized areas
  • Closures work; structure intact; no missing hardware
R/A ceiling — TRP-5 maximum if any third-party intervention.
TRP-6 Fair
Restricted

Heavy wear throughout. Functional but shows significant deterioration affecting presentation. Restricted acceptance — ultra-luxury iconic styles only (Hermès, Chanel classic lines, Louis Vuitton heritage, Goyard, Bottega Veneta intrecciato). All damage individually itemized.

  • Heavy corner wear with significant color loss or structural softening
  • Hardware: heavy oxidation; broken hardware acceptable if itemized
  • Handles: significant cracking, active peeling, or extreme patina altering shape
  • Interior: heavy staining or lining damage; persistent strong odor must be disclosed
  • Bag remains structurally intact and wearable without repair
Restricted — ultra-luxury iconic styles only.
Below gradable condition — object designations
DAM Damaged

The object exists but is not wearable without significant repair. Structural integrity is compromised, a component is missing or broken beyond function, or condition of materials makes the bag non-presentable. Recorded for completeness and provenance — not assigned a TRP grade. Endpoints: significant repair, upcycle, or parts use. A Damaged object that is subsequently repaired re-enters the grading process with a full R/A log.

Examples: broken frame preventing closure; missing or broken lock on a Hermès; complete lining separation; severe structural deformation; active mold.

DEC Decommissioned
Permanent

The object has been confirmed destroyed, rendered permanently non-functional, or removed from circulation. Record is permanently retained — cannot be relisted, transferred, or reassigned to Active. Provenance integrity function: if any item surfaces claiming the GNS identifier of a Decommissioned object, the record provides grounds to flag it as potentially fraudulent.

Definitions
Wear Inherent to Design (WID)

A physical change to the bag that is a direct structural consequence of how the object was designed and constructed, occurring regardless of use or storage practice. WID marks develop because the materials respond to their own design over time — not because a person has worn or handled the bag. WID marks do not lower condition grade but must be noted in submission condition notes and flagged WID.

Confirmed WID: interior indent from zipper-under-flap on a Chanel WOC; base sag on unstructured leather totes without base supports; handle slot wear on bags where handles rest against the body when stored; lining impression from rigid internal frame on bags without padding.

Not WID: chain impression on a Chanel Classic Flap — correct tissue storage prevents this. A mark must be mechanically unavoidable regardless of correct storage to qualify as WID.
Storage Wear

Passive change introduced by conditions of storage over time with no human use of the bag. Includes: press marks from tissue or box walls, light dust settlement, vachetta or vegetable-tanned leather ambient tone shift, minimal humidity-related texture change, minor compression from stacking or flat storage. Acceptable at TRP-2. Does not constitute use wear.

Handling Wear from Transfer

Minimal contact marks from mechanical movement of free-hanging or free-moving metal components (chains, hardware, clasps, feet) against each other or the bag body during handling, packaging, shipping, or display. Mechanically unavoidable. Not use wear. Acceptable at TRP-2.

Repaired / Altered Layer

R/A is not a condition grade. It is a data layer that runs alongside any condition grade. A bag is always graded on its current physical state first. R/A records what interventions occurred that shaped that state. The grade and the R/A log are independent but related fields.

When R/A applies
  • Handle or strap replacement (partial or full)
  • Lining replacement or repair
  • Hardware replacement (any piece or full set)
  • Edge paint or glazing reapplication
  • Leather cleaning, conditioning, or treatment by professional service
  • Dye, paint, or color application to exterior leather
  • Structural repair (stitch, seam, frame)
  • Interior cleaning or odor treatment by professional service
  • Any modification to original design

Curated resale platforms (TRR, Fashionphile, Rebag) largely self-select repaired or altered inventory out of intake. R/A data in The Repertory is most valuable for items circulating on eBay, Poshmark, 1stDibs dealers, and direct or private sale — the channels with the least existing transparency.

Cross-Platform Mapping

The Repertory scale is designed to be mappable to the condition terminology used by major resale platforms. The table below shows how each platform’s grades correspond to Repertory tiers. Ambiguous mappings are flagged ⚑.

TRP-4 Very Good Very Good Shows Wear Very Good Pre-owned Good ⚑ Very Good / Good ⚑ Very Good Very Good
TRP-5 Good Good Worn Good Pre-owned Good / Fair ⚑ Good Good Good
TRP-6 Fair Fair / As Is ⚑ Fair / Flawed ⚑ Fair Pre-owned Fair Fair Fair Fair
⚑ Excellent tier split — All platforms collapse TRP-2 Exceptional and TRP-3 Excellent into a single “Excellent” label. The unworn/used distinction is lost.
⚑ Fashionphile 2024 scale change — Fashionphile retired its Very Good, Good, and Pristine labels in 2024, replacing with Giftable, Excellent, Shows Wear, Worn, Fair, and Flawed. Older listings use the legacy scale.
⚑ eBay Feb 2025 three-tier collapse — eBay consolidated pre-owned condition into three labels (Pre-owned Excellent, Pre-owned Good, Pre-owned Fair), significantly reducing resolution for valuation use.
⚑ Vestiaire “Very Good” — straddles the TRP-3/TRP-4 boundary depending on category and item type. Cannot be mapped with precision without reviewing the listing.
⚑ TRR “As Is” — has no direct Repertory equivalent. Applies to items accepted with significant flaws that do not meet Fair standards. Closest to DAM (Damaged) depending on the item.
⚑ Fashionphile “Flawed” — has no direct Repertory equivalent. Items accepted with itemized significant flaws. May correspond to TRP-6 or DAM depending on severity.
⚑ 1stDibs — no enforced condition standard. Terminology is seller-applied and unverified. Use with caution in valuation contexts.
⚑ Poshmark — peer-graded, no platform enforcement. Condition labels reflect seller self-assessment and are highly variable in accuracy.
